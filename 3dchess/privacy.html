3D Chess Ultra Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Summary

3D Chess Ultra has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. To play online, the app gives you an account on our game server (run by Bom Sapo Studios on Cloudflare) with a random player name the game makes up for you. We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. You can delete your account and everything about it at any time from Settings in the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you do offline stays on your iPhone and is not sent to us: your settings (Customize, Accessibility, Notifications), saved games, Queen Blitz progress and pawns, Zombies! best runs, and which items you have unlocked.

What our game server keeps

The first time you use an online feature, the app creates an account on our server. It holds:

  • Your account: a random account id and your game-made player name (for example "ShinyRook359").
  • Sign-in links, only if you use them: the anonymous user id Apple gives us for Sign in with Apple (we don't ask Apple for your name or email), and your Game Center player id. These let you get your account back on a new iPhone.
  • Friends: your friends, friend requests, players you've blocked and challenges.
  • Games: the online games you play (moves, results, how each game ended), your ratings, rank tier and stats, and the games you are still in.
  • Notifications: your iPhone's push notification token and which notifications you want, so we can tell you when it's your move.
  • Network address: your device's IP address reaches our server whenever you play online. We don't store it. To stop one network creating hundreds of accounts, we keep a one-way scrambled value made from the address and the date, and delete it the next day.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, which processes these requests for us under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

What other players can see

Other players can see your player name, your rank tier and ratings, your stats and your games against them, and your moves in games you play together. Your friends can see when you're online. Players you block can't find you, see your profile or send you requests.

Apple services

  • Game Center: if you're signed in to Game Center, your ratings, Zombies! bests and achievements are posted to Game Center, where Apple handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

How long we keep it

  • Your account lasts until you delete it. A player name that hasn't been used for 90 days can be given to someone else, and you'll get a new one if you come back.
  • A finished game's room is removed a few minutes after it ends, and an unused room within 7 days. Your results and ratings stay with your account.

Deleting your account

Open Settings › Delete account in the app. This removes your account, name, sign-in links, friends, blocks, notifications, ratings and game history from our server, resigns any online game you're still in, and, if you used Sign in with Apple, cancels the game's link to your Apple ID. Other players keep their own results of games they played against you, with you shown only as "A player". Things stored on your iPhone stay until you delete the app. If you can't open the app, email us from the address below with your player name and we'll delete it for you.

Children's privacy

3D Chess Ultra is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players can't type messages or names, so nothing written by other players is shown in the game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Effective" date above before the version with the change is released.
