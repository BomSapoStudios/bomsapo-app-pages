3D Chess Ultra Support

3D Chess Ultra plays chess inside a cube: pieces move along all three axes, and every player starts on their own face. Play the computer, pass the phone around, play friends or anyone online, survive Zombies!, or clear the board in Queen Blitz puzzles.

Frequently asked questions

How do the pieces move?
Tap the ? button in any game for the rules of that mode. Each mode's guide also opens on its own the first time you play it.

Do I need an account?
No sign-up is needed. The first time you play online, the game gives you a made-up player name. You never type a name, email or password.

How do I keep my account on a new iPhone?
Sign in to Game Center, or use Sign in with Apple from Settings, and your name, friends and ratings come back on your new iPhone. You can also tap My code on the old device and type that code on the new one within 10 minutes.

I bought something and it's missing.
Purchases belong to your Apple ID. Open Customize or Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.

How do I turn notifications on or off?
Open Settings › Notifications. You can choose each kind (your move, friend requests, challenges and more) or switch them all off.

Someone is bothering me.
On the Friends screen, tap Block next to their name. Blocked players can't find you or send you requests. To report a player, email us their player name.

How do I delete my account?
Open Settings › Delete account. This removes your account and online data from our server. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is removed.

I found a bug or have feedback. How do I reach you?
Email us at support@bomsapostudios.com. Telling us your iPhone model and the version number from the bottom of the Settings screen helps.
