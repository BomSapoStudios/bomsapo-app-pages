DigiTalis Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026 (DigiTalis 1.2 and later)

Summary

DigiTalis has no accounts or sign-in, and we (Bom Sapo Studios) do not collect or store any personal data on our own servers. Starting with version 1.2, DigiTalis shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google collects some information from your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.

What DigiTalis stores on your device

DigiTalis stores a small amount of information locally on your device, using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage, so the app remembers your preferences between sessions:

  • Your high scores for each difficulty mode and bubble speed
  • Your theme, difficulty, bubble speed, operator-input and Ad Continues preferences
  • A count of finished games, used to decide when to show a full-screen ad

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

DigiTalis uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show two kinds of ads: an optional video you can choose to watch when the water overflows, and a full-screen ad after some finished games. To deliver, measure and prevent fraud in these ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Information about ads you see and interact with, and about how you use the app
  • Crash and performance information about the ads SDK

Google handles this information under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether DigiTalis may track you across apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and ads are less personalized. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), DigiTalis shows Google's consent form before personalized ads are used.
  • Ad Continues: the main menu's Ad Continues switch turns off the optional overflow ad.

Data retention and deletion

You can clear DigiTalis's on-device data at any time with the "Reset High Scores" option on the start screen, or by deleting the app. Information collected by Google is retained under Google's policies; you can reset your device's advertising identifier in iOS Settings.

Children's privacy

DigiTalis is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Effective" date above before the version with the change is released.
