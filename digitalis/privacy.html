DigiTalis Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Summary

DigiTalis does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no network access, no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs. It works entirely offline.

What DigiTalis stores

DigiTalis stores a small amount of information locally on your device, using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage, so the app remembers your preferences between sessions:

  • Your high scores for each difficulty mode and bubble speed
  • Your selected difficulty, bubble speed, and operator-input preferences

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us, to any server, or to any third party. DigiTalis contains no networking code of any kind.

Third parties and SDKs

DigiTalis does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking software development kits (SDKs). No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party, because no data leaves your device in the first place.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you are always in control of it. You can clear it at any time by using the "Reset High Scores" option on the app's start screen, or by deleting the DigiTalis app from your device, which removes all locally stored data along with it.

Children's privacy

DigiTalis is suitable for users of all ages, including children, because it does not collect any personal data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of DigiTalis adds a feature that collects data — we will update this page and the "Effective" date above before that version is released.
